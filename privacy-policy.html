Privacy policy

Effective date: November 22, 2026

This policy explains how Sterling Commerce Solutions (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information submitted through sterlingcommercesolutions.com and its consultation inquiry process.

Information we collect

If you submit a consultation inquiry, we may collect your name, email address, service interest, business stage, Merchant Center status, approximate project budget, website URL and the project details you choose to provide. Please do not submit passwords, payment-card details, government identification, health information or other sensitive personal information through the inquiry form.

How we use information

We use inquiry information to respond to your request, assess whether a service may be appropriate, prepare a proposal, communicate about a potential engagement, protect the website and maintain business records. We do not sell inquiry information.

Service providers

We use third-party providers to operate the website and deliver business communications. These may include Vercel for website hosting and deployment, Resend for transactional email delivery, and GoDaddy for domain and mailbox services. Those providers process information as needed to provide their services under their own terms and privacy practices.

Website and technical information

Our hosting and security providers may process technical information such as IP address, browser type, requested pages and request timestamps for security, reliability and troubleshooting. Pages may load fonts or images from third-party content providers, which may receive ordinary browser request information.

Retention and security

We retain inquiry information for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, manage a potential or current business relationship, maintain appropriate records, resolve disputes and meet legal obligations. We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices and requests

You may ask us to correct or delete inquiry information, subject to records we must retain for legal, contractual or legitimate business reasons. Contact us at consultations@sterlingcommercesolutions.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our services and technology change. The effective date above will be updated when material revisions are published.

This page is a practical website privacy notice and should be reviewed by qualified counsel against the final business entity, data practices, applicable jurisdictions and any analytics or advertising tools added later.
