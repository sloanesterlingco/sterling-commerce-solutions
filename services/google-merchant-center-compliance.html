Merchant Center consulting

A clearer path through Merchant Center compliance.

A specialist review of your e-commerce website, business information, policies, product data and checkout—followed by a prioritized correction plan and agreed implementation.

Request a consultation

What the review covers

A Merchant Center review should look at the whole customer journey, not just one policy page. We compare what customers see on the website with the information supplied to Google and the actual way the store operates.

Business identity and transparency

We review the business name, contact details, ownership disclosures, website identity, checkout presentation and consistency across the storefront and Merchant Center account.

Policies and fulfillment details

We compare shipping times, costs, returns, refunds, cancellations and contact instructions across policy pages, product pages, checkout settings and merchant configuration. The written terms must match real operations.

Product data and checkout

We review product titles, descriptions, availability, pricing, landing-page accuracy, feed configuration and whether customers can complete the stated purchase flow.

A practical correction plan

You receive prioritized findings and an agreed scope for corrections. We document what was reviewed and what was changed so your team can maintain consistency after the engagement.

Common questions

Does a compliance review guarantee approval?

No. Google independently decides eligibility, verification, review outcomes and account enforcement. We provide professional assessment and agreed corrective work, not a guarantee.

What does the service cost?

Compliance review and repair engagements start at $2,000. The final fee depends on the store, account history, technical scope and amount of corrective work.

Can you review a suspended account?

We first inspect the exact status and available review or appeal options. We decline cases where Google has exhausted or prohibited further review opportunities.
