Misrepresentation assessment

When the notice is vague, the investigation has to be thorough.

A misrepresentation finding may not point to one specific page or sentence. We examine the connected details customers and Google can see, then identify inconsistencies worth correcting.

Request a consultation

Why the notice can be difficult to diagnose

A broad misrepresentation notice may not identify the exact element that raised concern. The work is therefore an evidence-led review of the business identity, website experience, product information and merchant settings—not a guess about one phrase.

The areas we investigate

We compare business identity and contact information, shipping and delivery promises, returns and refunds, payment and checkout disclosures, product availability, prices, landing pages and the Merchant Center feed.

Policies must describe the real business

Policy pages should be original to the merchant's actual operations, clearly written and consistent with the rest of the site. Reusing a template is not itself proof of a violation; contradictions, incomplete disclosures or inaccurate promises are what a careful review should look for.

Prior account history matters

Before accepting remediation, we require the full history of prior misrepresentation findings, review requests, appeals, suspensions and Google's responses. The remaining review options must be established before further work is proposed.

No circumvention and no approval promise

We do not create replacement accounts or domains to bypass enforcement. If Google has exhausted available review options, we decline advanced remediation. A genuinely new business requires a separate eligibility assessment and must comply with Google's rules.

Common questions

Does Google always explain exactly what caused misrepresentation?

Not necessarily. The notice may be broad. A structured review can identify inconsistencies to address, but it cannot claim certainty about Google's internal decision.

Does AI-written policy language automatically cause misrepresentation?

We do not assume that. Policies should be accurate, specific to the business and consistent with real operations. The focus is factual clarity, not trying to disguise how text was drafted.

What if my account has already been suspended?

We review the exact suspension notice and remaining appeal options first. If no further review is permitted, we do not offer an account-recovery engagement.
