Shopify storefront development

A storefront built around the business you want to create.

From a polished starting theme to a fully customized commerce experience, we build Shopify storefronts with thoughtful design, clear customer information and a structured handoff.

Request a consultation

A complete storefront foundation

We configure the visual structure, navigation, responsive layouts, product presentation, collections and key customer-facing pages. The goal is a coherent storefront that the owner can continue to develop.

Product and policy setup

We can prepare an initial product catalog and help write store-specific shipping, return, refund and privacy information based on the merchant's actual business model. The owner remains responsible for product accuracy, fulfillment and legal review.

Checkout and launch readiness

We configure the agreed Shopify checkout and storefront settings, review the customer journey and document the steps the owner needs to complete before accepting orders.

Merchant Center preparation is a separate scope

A well-built Shopify store is not automatically approved by Google. Merchant Center preparation, account-history review and submission assistance must be scoped separately. Google retains all approval and enforcement decisions.

Owner handoff and optional support

We provide a handoff covering store administration, product updates and the agreed integrations. Ongoing product sourcing, inventory, customer service and advertising are not included unless separately contracted.

Common questions

Can I bring my own domain and niche?

Yes. We can assess a customer-selected domain and build around the intended product category. Prior domain or Merchant Center history must be disclosed before any verification-related work.

Do you provide products or suppliers?

The storefront can be prepared with an agreed starter catalog, but the owner is responsible for inventory, sourcing, product compliance and fulfillment.

Can you guarantee Google approval?

No. Merchant Center approval is controlled by Google and is not guaranteed by storefront development.
