AI commerce technology

Make your storefront easier to navigate—with an intelligent guide.

An AI commerce concierge can help shoppers explore a catalog, understand product details and find the right next step, while keeping the merchant in control of the store and its policies.

Request a consultation

Guided product discovery

A concierge can help a shopper narrow a catalog by stated preferences, compare available product attributes and navigate to relevant product pages. Responses should be grounded in current, approved catalog data.

Store information and customer guidance

The assistant can explain published shipping, returns, sizing, care or service information and direct customers to the correct page. It should not invent policy exceptions or make promises the merchant has not approved.

Human handoff and boundaries

When a question requires judgment, account access or a customer-service decision, the assistant should route the shopper to the merchant's established support channel rather than improvise.

Integrations and governance

A project may connect product data, approved knowledge sources and selected store functions. Access should be limited to the minimum required; payment details, passwords and sensitive personal information should not be collected in a general chat.

Measurement and continuous improvement

We can define success measures such as product discovery engagement, assisted navigation and qualified support handoffs. AI usage, hosting, model and third-party service costs are identified before launch.

Common questions

Can the concierge place orders?

That depends on the selected integration and authorization design. We recommend beginning with read-only product discovery and clear links to the merchant's existing checkout.

Will it always give correct answers?

No AI system is infallible. We ground responses in approved information, set clear boundaries and provide a human handoff for uncertain or consequential questions.

Does it replace customer service?

No. It can help with routine discovery and published store information, but the merchant remains responsible for customer service and business decisions.
