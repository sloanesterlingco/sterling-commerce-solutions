Merchant Center field guide

A practical checklist for investigating a misrepresentation notice.

When a Merchant Center notice is broad, resist the urge to change random pages. Work through the customer journey and compare every important promise with the merchant account and actual operations.

Discuss your store

Start with the account history

Save the exact notice, date, affected account and domain. Collect every previous review request, appeal response and suspension notice. Confirm what review options Google currently makes available before changing anything or submitting another request.

Confirm business identity everywhere

Compare the legal or trading business name, address, customer-service contact, website identity and account details. Look for outdated business names, old contact details, mismatched domains or references to another store.

Read the policies as a customer would

Check that shipping, returns, refunds, cancellations and contact instructions are easy to find and accurately describe the real process. Remove contradictory wording and make sure policy details agree with Shopify settings and checkout.

Follow a product from search to checkout

Choose representative products. Compare feed title, description, price, availability, images and landing-page content. Continue through cart and checkout to identify unexpected costs, broken links or inconsistent delivery promises.

Check the whole customer journey

Review navigation, contact options, payment disclosures, shipping costs, delivery estimates, return costs and the actual checkout experience on mobile and desktop. A page can look polished while still containing a small but important contradiction.

Treat AI-generated text carefully—but accurately

Do not assume that AI-assisted writing is automatically a policy violation. The test is whether every statement is truthful, specific, complete and consistent. Human review is essential because a plausible-sounding policy can still describe the wrong business process.

Document corrections before any review request

Keep dated copies of changed pages, feed settings and relevant account notices. Record what was corrected and why. Only request a review when the current account status permits it and the store is genuinely ready.

Know when to stop

If Google has exhausted or prohibited further review options, do not attempt to evade the restriction with a replacement account or domain. Obtain qualified advice on the permitted options and avoid paying for remediation that cannot be performed.
