Important questions

Clarity, from the start.

Can you guarantee Merchant Center approval?

No. Google makes independent decisions about eligibility, verification, account reviews, and enforcement. Our services focus on a detailed review, accurate configuration, and agreed corrective work—not guaranteed approval.

What if my account was previously suspended?

We review prior account history and available appeal options before accepting remediation work. Accounts with no permitted review or appeal path are not eligible for advanced remediation, and we do not help circumvent platform restrictions.

Can I use my own domain name?

Yes. We can assess an existing domain or help configure a new one as part of an agreed development project. Domain history and merchant-account eligibility are reviewed before work starts.

Do you operate my store or run ads for me?

No. We build and configure commerce technology. Merchants remain responsible for products, fulfillment, customer operations, and advertising. We may introduce independent specialists when requested.

How much does a custom project cost?

Our standard storefront foundations start at $1,995. Extensive custom development, AI integrations, or advanced remediation are quoted separately after a discovery assessment.
