Important questions

Clarity, from the start.

Can I join a membership now?

Not yet. We are preparing the membership, billing, support terms and policies. You can inquire about launch updates; no membership charges are being collected through this site.

What is included in membership?

Membership support and research allowances will be defined before enrollment. Development work and third-party costs are separate.

Can you guarantee Merchant Center approval?

No. Google makes independent decisions about eligibility, verification, account reviews, and enforcement. Our services focus on a detailed review, accurate configuration, and agreed corrective work—not guaranteed approval.

What if my account was previously suspended?

We review prior account history and available appeal options before accepting remediation work. Accounts with no permitted review or appeal path are not eligible for advanced remediation, and we do not help circumvent platform restrictions.

Who owns my accounts?

You own your Shopify store, domain, Google accounts, and supplier relationships from the beginning. Our team works through authorized access. You enter banking, payment, identity, and tax details directly with the provider.

Can you help with the business setup checklist?

Yes. We guide you through business registration, EIN where applicable, local tax requirements, Shopify, domains, suppliers, payment setup, Merchant Center and Google Ads. Legal and tax filings remain the business owner’s responsibility.

Can I use my own domain name?

Yes. We can assess an existing domain or help configure a new one as part of an agreed development project. Domain history and merchant-account eligibility are reviewed before work starts.

Do you operate my store or run ads for me?

Store operations, advertising management and ongoing support may be available under separately scoped agreements. Unless explicitly included in your signed proposal, merchants remain responsible for products, fulfillment, customer operations and advertising.

How much does a custom project cost?

Our standard storefront foundations start at $1,995. Extensive custom development, AI integrations, or advanced remediation are quoted separately after a discovery assessment.
