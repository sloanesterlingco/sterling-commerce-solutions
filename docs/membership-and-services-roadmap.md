# Sterling Commerce Solutions — Membership and Services Website Roadmap

Status: planning draft, not approved for production publishing.

## Positioning
"You shouldn't have to figure out e-commerce alone." Explain the journey from an idea at the foot of an E-Commerce skyscraper to a clear staircase of milestones. Avoid promises of revenue or guaranteed approvals.

## Proposed membership
Founding price: $29/month, subject to approval and billing setup.
- Niche exploration and evaluation
- Searchable learning resources and practical checklists
- 24/7 AI commerce concierge for general guidance, with transparent limitations and escalation paths
- Introductory human consultation, with duration and frequency to be defined
- 10% discount on eligible Sterling Commerce Solutions professional service fees; exclude advertising budgets, domains, Shopify fees, supplier costs and other pass-through expenses
- Member dashboard with individualized milestones and questions

## Two customer paths
1. Help Me Build: guided DIY education and optional expert support.
2. Build It For Me: scoped turnkey storefront development, setup, supplier/product coordination, merchant readiness, handover and personalized training.

## Service catalog
- Storefront build and branding
- Domain and Shopify configuration
- Product and supplier research
- Google Merchant Center setup, policy review and troubleshooting (Google approval not guaranteed)
- AI storefront customer support installation (only claim functioning once implemented and tested)
- Analytics and advertising setup/management
- Fulfillment and VA introductions
- CRM, retention and ongoing optimization
- Optional 30-day launch assistance

## Proposed pages/components
1. Membership landing page: hero, skyscraper image, benefits, $29 price proposal, 10% discount explanation, FAQ and signup CTA pending billing.
2. Choose Your Path: DIY vs Done-for-You comparison.
3. Services catalog: transparent scope, eligibility for member discount, quote requests.
4. How It Works: Discover -> Plan -> Build -> Verify -> Launch -> Grow.
5. Concierge demo: bounded questions, no false claims of live integrations.
6. Selected Work: Eloveya currently a live storefront demonstration, not a finished GMC-approved case study.
7. About / Our Story: firsthand experience with expensive instruction that lacked hands-on support; avoid naming or disparaging other providers.
8. Support standards: issue diagnosis, guided action, verification, written resolution record, escalation and defined response times.

## Eloveya publication gate
Do not publish a completed case study or claim GMC product approval until the four products' approval/eligibility is independently verified. Separate domain/account verification, product eligibility, and actual sales outcomes. Do not change the four products during review without authorization.

## Pre-launch checks
- Membership pricing, payment and cancellation terms approved
- Consultation time limits and unit economics defined
- 10% discount exclusions and stacking rules published
- Support escalation and scope documented
- Website inquiry email deliverability confirmed
- Accessibility/mobile QA and privacy policies
- Owner approval before publishing new sales claims or activating billing

## Membership payments and legal readiness (research required)
- Compare Stripe Billing with Shopify subscription options for a non-retail membership website: platform compatibility, recurring fees, payment processing, tax handling, checkout, self-service cancellation, customer portal, dunning, webhooks, and access provisioning.
- No payment collection or live signup until provider selection, implementation, testing, and owner approval.
- Draft and legally review Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, Subscription/Billing/Cancellation/Refund Policy, AI Concierge Disclosure, and separate Professional Services Agreement / statement of work.
- Disclose membership recurring charge and renewal interval clearly before purchase, provide accessible cancellation, and confirm applicable automatic-renewal and consumer protection requirements.
- Explain how user questions, uploaded business documents, and any connected store data are handled, retained, and shared with AI/payment/service providers.
- Clearly define 10% discount eligibility, exclusions, duration, and non-stacking rules.
- Verify tax obligations, data security, cookie/analytics consent needs, and accessibility with qualified professionals as applicable.
