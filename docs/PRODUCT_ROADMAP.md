# Sterling Commerce Solutions — Product and Delivery Roadmap
Updated: 2026-11-27. Planning document; not a statement that all features are live.

## North star
Help solo entrepreneurs understand what to do, when to do it, and how to complete it. E-commerce is the initial specialty; expand to general business management only after validating workflows. Stacey is the customer-facing coordinator, backed by specialized AI and human experts.

## Current working assets / evidence
- GitHub static website with dedicated Home, Designs, Build Your Store, Memberships, Services, Our Work and Contact pages.
- Existing contact form has reportedly delivered a test email; reliability and production deployment require verification.
- Storefront gallery currently presents visual concepts, not operational Shopify demos.
- Eloveya is an owned demonstration/case study, not a paying client. Any Merchant Center approval figures or sales claims must be independently verified before publication.
- Stacey telephone receptionist, memberships/checkout, agentic operations and business document vault are NOT deployed.

## Customer-facing product lines
1. Commerce Launch: store design/build, domain, catalog, supplier/fulfillment, payment/shipping, Google Merchant Center, launch.
2. Marketing & Growth: Google Ads, Microsoft/Bing Ads, social media organic/paid, email retention, SEO, analytics.
3. AI Commerce: AI product discoverability, product-feed readiness, referral attribution; agentic marketing automation with human approvals.
4. Sterling Business Navigator: guided business formation, operational checklist, compliance calendar, financial organization, accountant handoff.
5. Future managed services: specialists supervise AI workflows in commerce, finance operations, compliance, marketing, customer support.

## Business Navigator modules — proposed
- Guided onboarding: jurisdiction, city, business activity, entity/tax status, employees, sales channels; distinguish verified requirements from educational guidance.
- Formation: name, entity filings, DBA, EIN, licenses, state tax registration; official links, customer submission, licensed-professional referral where necessary.
- Recurring obligations: monthly reconciliation, P&L, quarterly estimated-tax review where applicable, state-specific sales tax, annual reports, license renewals, payroll obligations.
- Document vault: Firebase Auth + Firestore metadata + Cloud Storage tenant isolation; file validation, malware scanning, audit logs, encryption, deletion/retention, user consent and controlled AI processing.
- Accountant package: indexed ZIP of source records, reviewed financial workbook, payment history, outstanding questions; no automatic tax return preparation or filing in initial version.
- Integrations later: Shopify, QuickBooks/Xero, ad platforms, banking through approved integrations, calendar, email.
- Human oversight: qualified accountant/legal review for individualized tax/legal issues; no claims of guaranteed government approvals.

## Prioritized delivery
### Now — website experience and validation
- Distinct page heroes and clean nav; verify production deployment and mobile responsiveness.
- Interactive staircase with business tasks and clear CTA to Business Navigator preview.
- Interactive Business Navigator discovery/checklist prototype (educational, no sensitive documents or accounts).
- Contact form delivery, success/error feedback, spam protection, analytics; reconcile inconsistent package prices.
- Full Eloveya case study with verified evidence; replace visual concepts with genuine storefront demos.
### Next — paid membership MVP
- Define membership benefits and prices; checkout, identity, member access.
- Save business profile/checklist progress in authenticated database; configurable reminders and audit history.
- Jurisdiction-specific requirements from verified official sources, with review date and uncertainty flags.
### Later — secure financial workspace
- Security/privacy review and access-control tests before accepting financial or tax documents.
- Document ingestion, categorization with confirmation, exports, accountant permissions.
- Professional-supervised integrations and automation.
### Future — AI receptionist and managed business operations
- Retell voice agent and phone number; test disclosures, escalation, summaries, privacy.
- AI marketing and business operations only after measurable pilots and explicit action approvals.

## Critical guardrails
- Revenue != profit. No guaranteed AI recommendations, advertising results, tax outcomes or Merchant Center approval.
- No customer SSNs, driver's licenses, banking credentials or tax returns through prototype forms.
- No legal/tax advice presented as individualized professional advice.
- No spending, filings or external submissions without authorization.
- Preserve customer ownership of Shopify, ad, domain and government accounts.
- Avoid saying a planned feature is live; clearly label demos and examples.

## Immediate acceptance checks
1. Home stair interaction keyboard/touch usable; reduced motion respected.
2. Navigator preview explains the scope and does not claim to determine legal obligations.
3. All page routes and contact form function on production, including mobile.
4. Product and marketing copy matches actual implemented services.
